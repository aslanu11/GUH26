### NatWest Challenge: AI-Powered Financial Inclusion and Resilience

- Core brief: design an AI-driven solution promoting financial fairness and social mobility
- Target users: hard-pressed families and underserved communities facing cost of living pressures
  - Rising costs: food, energy, housing
  - Vulnerable groups face greatest barriers to fair financial services
- “Customer back” mindset: start with customer need, then technology
- Solution framed as a trusted AI financial coach
- Key capabilities to build toward:
  - Personalised budgeting and financial planning
  - Predictive insights and future trend forecasting (“financial time travel”)
  - Proactive, tailored advice before hardship hits
  - Accessibility for underserved and vulnerable communities

### NatWest Judging Criteria

- Customer focus and user-centric design
  - Does it address real needs? Is it accessible and personalised?
- Innovation and creativity
  - Novel use of AI to promote financial inclusion
- Social impact and inclusivity
  - Promotes fairness, ethical and transparent approach
- Use of AI and data
  - Responsible, value-adding use of predictive tools
- Feasibility and practicality
  - Realistic within current or near-future financial ecosystem
- Judges evaluate impact and idea, not code quality: “smoke and mirrors” demo is fine
